# The verdict, #37 criterion 2

A comment on #26 that names the chosen design and says why. It is the gate #16
never had: nothing in phase two is written before it, and it is Fran's, not an
agent's.

Post it with the branch's own credential:

```
gh issue comment 26 -F <file>
```

## What it has to carry

The criterion is "names the chosen variant or the combination, and says why".
Two things, and the second is what the phase-two tickets are written from — a
verdict that names a winner without reasons leaves the breakdown guessing which
qualities to preserve.

A combination is named part by part against the variant it comes from, because
that is what the tickets will be cut along: hero, experience list, recognitions
band, contact, masthead, type, palette.

## The shape

> **Verdict on #26: <the variant, or the combination>.**
>
> <Which design gets built. A single variant by key and name, or the parts and
> where each comes from.>
>
> **Why.** <What in the pass decided it — what it does for a recruiter who
> already has the name, and what the runners-up did worse.>
>
> **What does not carry over.** <Anything in the chosen variant that the build
> should drop or change. Optional, and worth a line where the pass turned some
> up.>
>
> The prototype is captured on `prototype/26-five-variants`; see the pointer
> above.
